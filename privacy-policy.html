1.0 Corporate Overview & Scope

People Network Management LLP ("PNM", "we", "us", or "our") is a registered Limited Liability Partnership operating in India. We operate as an integrated HR solutions partner delivering end-to-end Payroll & Statutory Compliance Processing, Recruitment Consultancy, and HR Software Implementation (Payroll Cell HRMS).

This Privacy Policy sets out our corporate commitment, operational standards, and legal safeguards regarding the collection, handling, storage, transfer, and deletion of personal and corporate data obtained through:

  • Our official public website (pnmindia.com) and digital inquiry portals (including forms, WhatsApp redirect triggers, and readiness assessment calculators);
  • Our client service engagements where employer organizations transmit payroll, employee master, and attendance inputs to PNM;
  • Our Recruitment & Executive Search practice where candidate resumes, profiles, and evaluation records are processed;
  • Our proprietary and partner HRMS software implementation layer (Payroll Cell) and connected biometric/attendance portals.

2.0 Applicable Legal & Regulatory Framework

PNM complies with all applicable privacy, employment, and information security laws governing businesses in the Republic of India, specifically:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act): Governing fair and transparent processing of digital personal data;
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011;
  • Statutory Indian Labour Laws: Including the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952; the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948; the Payment of Wages Act, 1936; the Minimum Wages Act, 1948; and state-specific Professional Tax (PT) and Labour Welfare Fund (LWF) enactments;
  • Income Tax Act, 1961: In respect of Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), salary tax computation, and Form 16 issuance.

Statutory Confidentiality Guarantee

PNM treats all employee salary registers, gross-to-net calculations, bank account disbursement files, and PAN/Aadhaar records as strictly confidential corporate and personal assets. Such data is never shared, marketed, or monetized.

3.0 Processor vs. Data Fiduciary Roles

To understand how your data is handled, please note the distinct operational roles PNM assumes:

  • When PNM acts as a "Data Processor": In our Payroll Outsourcing, Statutory Compliance, and Payroll Cell HRMS implementations, the employing organization (our client) is the Data Fiduciary. PNM processes employee master data, biometric records, leaves, and salary computations strictly upon the documented instructions and service contract executed with the employer.
  • When PNM acts as a "Data Fiduciary": PNM acts as the direct fiduciary when you visit our website, submit a consultation or payroll review request, apply for an internal position at PNM via careees@pnmindia.com, or engage directly with our advisory consultants.

4.0 Data We Collect & Receive

Depending on your relationship with PNM, we collect and process the following categories of information:

A. Website Inquiries & Interactive Tools

When you fill our contact form, request a payroll review, book a Payroll Cell demo, or submit data via our WhatsApp integration, we collect:

  • Full Name, Corporate Email Address, Phone/Mobile Number, Company/Entity Name;
  • Employee Headcount range and primary area of interest (Payroll, HRMS, Recruitment, or Integrated);
  • Specific business challenges, implementation timelines, and notes provided in inquiry textareas.

B. Corporate Payroll & Compliance Records (Client Engagements)

Under formal client contracts, client HR administrators securely provide:

  • Employee Identifiers: Employee code, full name, date of birth, gender, marital status, permanent and current address, contact details;
  • Statutory & Tax KYC: Permanent Account Number (PAN), Aadhaar number (handled strictly under UIDAI masking standards), Universal Account Number (UAN), PF Number, ESIC IP number;
  • Financial & Salary Details: Bank name, account number, IFSC code, CTC salary structure, basic, HRA, special allowances, reimbursements, bonus, and deduction parameters;
  • Time & Attendance Data: Biometric logs, punch records, shift rosters, overtime hours, leaves applied/approved, and loss of pay (LOP) inputs.

C. Recruitment & Candidate Profiles

For candidates seeking employment opportunities through our Recruitment practice:

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Resumes, educational credentials, work history, skill certifications;
  • Current compensation, expected CTC, notice period, location preferences, and recruiter interview notes.

5.0 Purpose of Data Processing

We process personal and organizational data solely for legitimate business purposes:

  1. Accurate Payroll Processing: Computing monthly gross-to-net salaries, arrears, overtime, deductions, bonus, and producing official payslips;
  2. Statutory Compliance Mandates: Generating Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) for PF, monthly ESIC contribution challans, PT filings, and quarterly TDS 24Q returns;
  3. Salary Disbursement Advisory: Compiling bank-compatible NEFT/RTGS salary disbursement advice files for corporate bank accounts;
  4. HRMS Platform Deployment: Configuring workflows, approval matrices, and Employee Self Service (ESS) portals in Payroll Cell;
  5. Talent Placement: Matching qualified candidates with client job descriptions and coordinating interview/offer lifecycles;
  6. Customer Support & Audits: Responding to client inquiries, statutory labour inspections, and internal reconciliation audits.

7.0 Statutory Disclosures & Authorized Third Parties

PNM does not sell, lease, or distribute confidential client or employee data. Disclosures are made strictly to:

  • Government & Statutory Bodies: Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), state Labour Commissioners, and the Income Tax Department as obligated by law;
  • Scheduled Commercial Banks: Disbursing banks specified by the client for processing direct salary credits via secure corporate banking APIs;
  • Auditors & Legal Counsel: Independent Chartered Accountants or legal advisors bound by professional non-disclosure agreements during statutory compliance audits;
  • Law Enforcement Authorities: Exclusively where served with a valid court order, warrant, or governmental directive under Indian jurisdiction.

8.0 Security, Encryption & Technical Safeguards

PNM deploys enterprise-grade administrative, technical, and physical security measures:

  • Encryption in Transit: 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption across all web traffic, client upload portals, and API transfers;
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict principle of least privilege. Only certified payroll specialists assigned to a specific client account can access corresponding employee rosters;
  • Immutable Audit Trails: Every change, recalculation, export, or print command in our payroll engines is timestamped with user credentials;
  • Clean Desk & Screen Policy: Strict operational guidelines enforced at our corporate delivery centre preventing unauthorized recording or screen capture of salary masters;
  • Regular Data Backups: Geographically resilient, encrypted backups maintained with daily point-in-time recovery capabilities.

9.0 Data Retention & Safe Deletion

Upon termination of a client service agreement and completion of the Full & Final (F&F) transition handover:

  1. PNM transfers complete historical payroll archives and statutory registers to the client's authorized representative;
  2. Active databases are decommissioned, and historical backups are retained strictly for the mandatory statutory period (e.g., 8 years under the Income Tax Act for tax audit verification);
  3. Following the expiration of statutory requirements, electronic records are securely sanitized and permanently purged from our infrastructure.

10.0 Data Subject Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, data principals hold specific rights regarding their digital personal information:

  • Right to Access: The right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed;
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: The right to request the correction of inaccurate data or the completion of incomplete records;
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: The right to register a grievance with our Data Protection Officer regarding any processing irregularity;
  • Right to Nominate: The right to nominate an individual who will exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Note for Corporate Employees: As an outsourced processor, employee requests regarding salary changes or employer-held records should be directed to your employer's HR department, who will authorize PNM to execute corresponding updates.

11.0 Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website utilizes minimal, essential session cookies and performance telemetry to maintain security, preserve mobile drawer states, and analyze aggregated traffic trends. We do not use third-party invasive behavioral tracking or advertising cookies.

You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though doing so may slightly impact the responsiveness of certain interactive tools such as our HR Readiness Calculator.
