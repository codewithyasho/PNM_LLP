2.0 Scope of Integrated Services & SOW Framework

PNM operates as an integrated people management partner rather than a transactional staffing agency. Our operations are structured across three interconnected service pillars:

  • Pillar 1: Managed Payroll & Statutory Compliance: Comprehensive monthly compensation processing, component salary structuring, overtime and arrears calculations, bonus and incentive management, statutory deductions (PF, ESIC, PT, LWF, TDS), automated payslip generation, and Full & Final (F&F) settlement administration.
  • Pillar 2: Talent Acquisition & Recruitment Services: Sector-specific executive search, permanent placement, lateral hiring, and specialized recruitment across Information Technology, Healthcare & Hospital Administration, Manufacturing & Heavy Engineering, and Corporate Business Support Functions.
  • Pillar 3: Payroll Cell HRMS Software Implementation: Digital HR transformation, software customization, multi-location biometric attendance integration, ERP/accounting connector deployment, employee self-service (ESS) portal configuration, and ongoing cloud support.

3.0 Client Responsibilities & Payroll Cutoff Protocols

The timely and error-free execution of payroll and statutory compliance depends upon timely, validated, and authorized data inputs furnished by the Client. The Client agrees to adhere strictly to the following operational protocols:

  • Designated Single Point of Contact (SPOC): The Client shall designate authorized HR/Finance representatives authorized to approve master employee changes, variable pay inputs, and salary registers.
  • Monthly Cutoff Deadlines: Raw inputs including biometric attendance sheets, approved leaves, overtime logs, variable incentives, salary revisions, new hire onboarding masters, and resignation records must be delivered to PNM by the mutually agreed cutoff date (typically the 24th or 25th calendar day of each pay cycle).
  • Verification & Authorization: Following delivery of the preliminary Payroll Register and Bank Disbursement Advice by PNM, the Client must review, audit, and provide formal written sign-off within 24 to 48 hours to enable timely fund transfers.
  • Data Accuracy Indemnity: PNM computes payroll strictly based upon Client-supplied attendance, salary structures, and declarations. PNM bears no liability for delayed salary disbursements, incorrect deductions, or banking rejections resulting from inaccurate or delinquent Client submissions.

5.0 Statutory Compliance Mandates & Regulatory Disclaimer

PNM provides expert administrative, calculation, and reporting support across Indian labor enactments, including but not limited to:

  • The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 (EPFO)
  • The Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 (ESIC)
  • State-specific Professional Tax (PT) and Labour Welfare Fund (LWF) Acts
  • The Income Tax Act, 1961 (Section 192 TDS salary tax computation, Form 24Q, Form 16)
  • The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and The Payment of Bonus Act, 1965
  • Minimum Wages Act, 1948 and Code on Wages mandates
Statutory Employer Responsibility Disclaimer

While PNM prepares accurate challans, electronic returns (ECRs), and filings, the Client remains the principal employer of record under Indian law. The Client is solely responsible for funding and maintaining active banking balances to pay the designated government challans on or before the statutory due dates (e.g., 15th of every month for PF/ESIC). PNM is not liable for statutory interest, damages, or penalties arising from the Client's failure to release challan funds on schedule.

7.0 Recruitment & Talent Acquisition Engagement Terms

Recruitment advisory services provided by PNM are conducted under clear professional standards and industry-recognized engagement frameworks:

  • Candidate Representation Validity: Resumes and profiles introduced to the Client by PNM remain valid representations for twelve (12) months from the date of profile transmission. If the Client hires an introduced candidate within this period for any role, PNM's standard placement fee applies.
  • Fee Structure & Payment Schedule: Placement fees are computed as an agreed percentage of the candidate's first-year Total Gross Cost to Company (CTC). Invoices are raised on the date of candidate joining and are payable within fifteen (15) to thirty (30) days as stated in the SOW.
  • Replacement Guarantee: In the event a placed candidate voluntarily resigns or is terminated for valid cause within sixty (60) to ninety (90) days of joining, PNM will deploy reasonable commercial efforts to provide one (1) suitable replacement candidate at no additional consultancy charge, provided all recruitment invoices were cleared within agreed credit terms.
